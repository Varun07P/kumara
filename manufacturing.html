From our 5,000+ sq. ft. manufacturing facility in Bengaluru, Kumara Engineering Solutions (KES) designs, fabricates, powders coats, wires, and tests custom LV & MV switchgear boards for industries, IT parks, hospitals, and infrastructure projects across Karnataka.

Every switchboard is built using heavy-gauge CRCA sheet steel (14/16 SWG), 9-tank pre-treatment, automated electrostatic powder coating (Siemens Grey RAL 7032), and high-conductivity 99.97% electrolytic copper/aluminium busbars.

Quality Lifecycle

6-Stage Manufacturing Process

Every panel is built through a disciplined, trackable workflow ensuring zero errors upon site delivery.

01

SLD Review & Costing

Thorough electrical requirement analysis, BOQ estimation, and commercial acknowledgement.

02

GA & Schematic CAD

Complete General Arrangement (GA) drawings and power schematics for client engineering sign-off.

03

OEM Sourcing

Procurement of Tier-1 switchgear (ABB, Schneider, L&T, Siemens) with incoming FAT checks.

04

CNC Fabrication

Precision CRCA cutting, 9-tank pre-treatment, powder coating (RAL 7032) & copper busbar forming.

05

100% FAT Testing

Point-to-point wiring tests, HV insulation dielectric checks, and full functional relay simulations.

06

Site Handover

Protective packaging, on-site positioning, CEIG trial runs, test certificates, and warranty handover.

Calibrated Testing Lab

In-House Quality Inspection Tools

All panels undergo rigorous factory acceptance testing (FAT) using certified, NABL-calibrated testing instruments before dispatch.

HV Dielectric Test

High voltage dielectric strength testing up to 5kV per IEC 61439 standards.

1000V / 500V Megger

Insulation resistance verification across phase-to-phase and phase-to-earth circuits.

Earth Resistance Test

Dedicated Earth Testers ensuring body grounding integrity < 1.0 Ohm.

Secondary Current Injection

Protection relay tripping timing and breaker secondary current injection verification.

Torque & Conductivity

Calibrated torque wrenches on all busbar joints and micro-ohm conductivity checks.

Phase Sequence & Tong Tester

Digital Multimeters, Tong Testers, and phase sequence meters for 3-phase balancing.

Paint Thickness & Gloss

Magnetic dry film thickness (DFT) gauges and scratch adhesion verification.

Temperature Rise Rig

Calibrated current injection test facility for thermal verification up to 4000A.
